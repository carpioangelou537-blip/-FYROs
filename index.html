Our Fire Safety Services.

We provide complete fire protection equipment, installation, and maintenance services to keep your facility and people safe.

01
PORTABLE EXTINGUISHERS

Our staff installs and maintains the majority of portable fire extinguishers. We perform annual inspections and ensure damaged units are repaired or replaced. Professional handling and training included.

02
FIXED FIRE SYSTEMS

Specialized fixed systems including fire detection & alarm (FDAS), kitchen suppression systems (KSS), and fire sprinkler systems (FSSS). Professional installation and code compliance monitoring.

03
SMOKE ALARMS

Professional smoke alarm installation for residential and commercial properties. Proper placement, testing, and maintenance to ensure early fire detection and prevention.

04
CCTV INSTALLATION

Complete CCTV system installation and monitoring for enhanced facility security. Professional setup ensuring code compliance and operational reliability at all times.
